{{ site.author.name }}

I study the Arctic stratospheric polar vortex, the ozone loss associated with it, and severe convective weather.

I completed my PhD in Meteorology at Leipzig University in April 2026 (magna cum laude). In November 2026 I join the Department of Meteorology (MISU) at Stockholm University as a postdoctoral fellow in stratospheric dynamics and turbulence.

My work focuses on the variability, morphology and extreme states of the polar vortex, in particular exceptionally strong vortex events, and on the dynamical controls of Arctic ozone. Before my PhD, I worked on WRF modelling of thunderstorms, lightning, hailstorms and heavy rainfall over South Asia. I combine reanalysis (ERA5), climate-model output (CMIP), ICON and WRF simulations, and explainable machine learning. More on my research.
